Development of an easy-to-use, robust, and modern mobile application and website for inventory management, designed to help the company track its products and materials in real-time. The features include:
📈 Insight dashboard for a quick and comprehensive overview of stocks.
👪 Customer and supplier management for better collaboration and optimal supply management.
🔏 Multi-user access for enhanced security and more efficient permission management.
🧾 Order management with assignment of delivery trucks and printing of delivery notes (POS) for optimal traceability of goods flow.
↩️ Return management for smooth handling of defective or excess stock products.
🔔 Notifications for quick and effective communication with users.

  • CSS3

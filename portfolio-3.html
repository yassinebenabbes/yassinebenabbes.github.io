Description

  • Yassine ben abbes

Development and implementation of an e-learning platform that allows:
👪 User management (administrators, students, teachers, parents).
🔏 Privilege management.
💬 Connections: Facilitating communication between users (inter-application communication via messages and video calls).
🧾 Quiz management.
📅 Online meetings: Managing online meetings based on Zoom's API.
🛢 Reference table: Managing levels, time slots, subjects, and attendance.
📈 Dashboard.
🔔 Notifications.
⚙️ General settings (name, company name, logo of the organization, contact information).

Technology

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • PHP5
  • LARAVEL
  • VUEJS
